Events & Roadmap

From kickoff to commercial verification — follow OpenAN's journey as we build the open foundation for autonomous telecom networks.

/ Roadmap Timeline

OpenAN launched at MWC-Barcelona in March 2026 and open sourced its seed code, donating to LF Networking in June 2026 at DTW-Copenhagen. Commercial verification is planned through 2027.

2026.03 MWC Barcelona

Kickoff (Launch)

China Mobile and Huawei announce OpenAN; development and verification begin in earnest. The project introduces the vision for closed-loop autonomous networks with open, vendor-neutral standards.

Launch Announcement
2026.06 DTW Copenhagen

Seed Code & LFN Donation

A2A-T SDK, Registry Center, and Orchestration Center are open sourced and donated to LF Networking. The three seed components become available under Apache 2.0 license.

Open Source LFN Donation Apache 2.0
2026.12 TM Forum IHPP

Launch Innovations

Scenario solution packages and more contributors join; OpenAN is positioned as an Innovation Hub Pioneer Project. Joint innovation with operators produces scenario-based solutions.

Innovation Hub Scenario Packages
2027 Commercial Verification

Commercial Verification

Startup member review, ongoing maintenance, and commercial verification of OpenAN deployments by operators. Real-world validation across multiple operator environments.

Production Validation
