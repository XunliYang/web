Membership

OpenAN is a vendor-neutral, open community of operators, vendors, and innovators building the future of autonomous telecom networks. Our members span the entire telecom ecosystem.

OpenAN brings together the world's leading telecom organizations to define, build, and verify closed-loop autonomous network standards. Every member contributes to a shared vision — and benefits from shared progress.
