Leadership

The OpenAN Leadership team brings together industry experts and thought leaders from leading organizations to guide the strategic direction of the project toward Autonomous Networks Level 5.

OpenAN governance follows the LF Networking model with a Leadership Committee for project-level decisions. The committee oversees strategic direction, membership, and overall project governance.

/ Governance Model

Leadership Committee

Project-level decisions including strategy, membership approval, and charter evolution. Includes Chair, Vice-Chair, and Voting Members.

Technical Steering Committee

Technical direction, architecture decisions, and code quality oversight. The TSC Chair guides technical discussions and decision-making.

View TSC Members

Contributors & Maintainers

Individuals who contribute code, documentation, and reviews. Maintainers are responsible for specific repositories and components.

Read the OpenAN Charter to understand governance, scope, and contribution guidelines.
